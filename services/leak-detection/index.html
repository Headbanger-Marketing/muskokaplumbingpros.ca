Questions About the Work

Who approves opening a wall once a leak is traced?

Checks that avoid openings may come first, and the written limit should say when the provider must stop and ask you.

  • Note where moisture appears and what was in use

    Before anything is opened, the respondent should learn where the stain or drip appears, when it was first seen and which fixtures were running at the time. Photograph the area with something in frame for scale, add a reading from the water meter if you can take one safely, and name who can let the provider into each room.

  • Compare a simple check with opening the area

    Compare non-invasive checks at your Muskoka property with opening the area, and ask what finding from those checks would justify the second step. Agree in writing that nothing is opened until the provider has sent findings and you or your named contact have replied.

  • Ask for findings, photos and drying notes

    The respondent should give a written account of what was checked, where moisture was and was not found, and the photos that show it, sent to the person who authorizes the work. If a repair is made, ask what was done and whether any area still needs time to dry.
